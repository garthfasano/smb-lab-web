Where the money goes

The Lead Isn't The Problem. The Next Hour Is.

Most missed revenue happens between a lead arriving and a person being free to work it. In a busy location, that's every day.

  • A new lead waits hours for a reply, and books with whoever called first.
  • A quote goes out and nobody follows up after the second try.
  • The phone rings during a job and the caller never tries again.
  • Corporate can't see which locations are working their leads and which aren't.
  • When bookings slip, the lead source gets the blame, and the client starts shopping for a new agency.
