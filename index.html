FAQ

Common
Questions.

Does this replace my salesperson?

No. It makes your salesperson, and you, more effective. It handles the follow-up, notes, reminders and prep work, so your people spend their time talking to customers, and you spend less time managing.

Do I have to set it up myself?

No. We set it up with you: we connect your CRM, phone system and email, write your business info and voice together, and run every agent on sample customers before it touches a real one. No tech skills needed.

Does $99 really include call reviews and coaching?

Yes. Growth includes every agent that runs on your CRM and phone system, including call notes, call coaching, the morning brief and the daily scoreboard. Pro adds agents that need extra connections, like your books, calendars and ad accounts.

Does it work for my kind of business?

We built it in our own in-home service business, and it fits any business that sells by phone, text and quote: HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, home care, landscaping, contractors and more. You teach it your services, your process and your voice.

Will it text my customers on its own?

Only if you tell it to. Every agent starts in draft mode, and you choose per agent whether it waits for your approval or sends on its own. Bulk messages always wait for your approval.

Which CRMs does it work with?

GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Keap, Jobber and Housecall Pro. Most connect with an API key you paste into the client portal. Jobber connects by signing in.

Which phone systems work with it?

Dialpad, OpenPhone, RingCentral, Aircall, CallRail and Twilio. You can use one alongside any CRM. Calls and texts come from the phone system, and notes and appointments stay in your CRM.

My CRM can't send texts or emails. Will it still work?

Yes. Connect a phone system and texts go out from your business number. Connect Gmail or Outlook and emails send from your own address, with a copy sent to your CRM so the history stays in one place.

Do we need Slack?

No. You and your team chat with your assistant right in the client portal, with separate threads for different topics. If your team already lives in Slack, we can add it to a private channel there too.

How are my sales calls handled?

If your CRM or phone system records calls, each recording is transcribed once so the assistant can write notes and a follow-up. Recordings are only used for that.

We sell over Zoom, Google Meet or Teams. Does that work?

Yes. Connect the notetaker you already use, Fathom or Fireflies. Each finished meeting is matched to the customer in your CRM, gets a note, and gets a follow-up draft after a quote.

How do the $99 blocks work?

Each block covers about 100 new leads a month for a typical business. You get a heads-up at 80%, and if you hit your cap, leads already in progress keep getting help. Add another block anytime.

What is High Volume for?

Busier businesses working well past 100 leads a month. You get everything in Pro, with pricing built around your volume instead of $99 blocks. Teams with their own AI provider account (Anthropic, OpenAI) can plug it in and be billed by their provider directly.

Am I locked in?

No. Month to month. Cancel any time.
