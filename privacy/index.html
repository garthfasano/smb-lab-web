Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SMB Lab LLC ("SMB Lab," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information through our website at joinsmblab.com, our client portal at app.joinsmblab.com, our Slack workspace, our text messaging, and the AI agents we run for our customers (together, the "Services").

The short version

  • We use your data to run SMB Lab for you. We don't sell it and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use customer data to train AI models, and our AI providers don't train on it either.
  • For Gmail we only ask permission to send email. We don't read your inbox.
  • Our customers control the data about their own leads and clients. We process it for them.
  • Questions or requests: support@joinsmblab.com.

1. Our two roles

When you visit our site or sign up, SMB Lab decides how your information is used and is responsible for it as described here.

When we run agents for a business (our "Customer"), we process information about that business's leads, clients, staff and conversations on the Customer's behalf and under its instructions. That information is "Customer Data." The Customer is responsible for it, including for having the right to share it with us and to contact the people involved. If you're a lead or client of a business that uses SMB Lab and want to make a request about your information, please contact that business. We'll help them respond, and you can also write to us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and contact details: name, business name, email, phone number, login credentials, and the people you invite.
  • Billing: plan, billing contact and payment history. Card details are collected and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. We don't store full card numbers.
  • Business setup: the business information, services, pricing, policies, voice and instructions you give your agents.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, text, Slack or portal chat, demo requests, and support requests.

Information from accounts you connect

When a Customer connects its systems, we access the data needed for the agents to work. Depending on what's connected, this can include contacts, leads, notes, tags, pipelines and appointments from a CRM; call recordings, call logs and texts from a phone system; meeting transcripts from a notetaker such as Fathom or Fireflies; sending access to Gmail or Outlook; and calendar availability and events. We store API keys and access tokens encrypted.

Information created by the Services

Transcripts, call notes, summaries, follow-up drafts, coaching notes and scores, reports, agent activity logs and portal conversation history.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our servers and hosting providers record standard log data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and dates and times. The portal uses cookies that are needed to keep you signed in and secure. We don't currently use advertising cookies or third-party ad trackers on our site. Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font is delivered.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Services: running agents, writing notes and drafts, sending the messages you approve or allow, preparing reports, and showing your data in Slack and the portal.
  • To set up, support and secure accounts, including preventing fraud and abuse.
  • To bill you and manage your subscription.
  • To communicate with you about your account, the Services, and, if you agree, our products. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time.
  • To improve SMB Lab. We learn from how the Services perform (for example by fixing errors, improving agent instructions and workflows, and measuring quality), using usage data and de-identified or aggregated information where possible.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. AI processing

Our agents use AI models from providers such as Anthropic and OpenAI (for example to transcribe calls and to write notes, drafts and summaries). We send these providers only the information needed for the task.

  • We don't use Customer Data to train or fine-tune AI models, ours or anyone else's.
  • Our AI providers process this data under business API terms that don't allow them to train their models on it.
  • Customers who have agreed with us in writing may use their own AI provider accounts. In that case, the Customer's agreement with that provider also applies.

5. Google user data

Customers can connect a Google account so their agents can send email from Gmail and work with Google Calendar. This section explains exactly what we access and how we treat it.

What we access

  • Gmail, send only. We request permission only to send email on your behalf. We use it to send the follow-ups and other emails you approve or allow your agents to send, from your own address, with a copy (BCC) to the CRM logging address you provide. We do not request access to read, search or delete your email.
  • Google Calendar. We read your calendars' events and free/busy times to check availability and avoid conflicts, and we create or update events when you or your agents book or change appointments.
  • Basic account information. Your name and email address, to identify which Google account is connected.

How we use it

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you've turned on: sending your emails, checking availability, and booking appointments. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

How we share it

We don't share Google user data with third parties, except: with the AI providers described in Section 4, only as needed to perform the feature you requested (for example, checking calendar availability to suggest appointment times); with our hosting provider, which stores it for us; when you direct us to (for example, the BCC copy to your CRM); to comply with law; or as part of a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

Human access

Our team doesn't read your Google user data unless you give us permission for a specific message or event (for example, for a support request), it's needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it's required by law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Storage and deletion

Google access tokens are stored encrypted. Calendar information is cached only as long as needed to provide scheduling features. We keep records of emails we sent for you (such as recipient, subject, body and send time) in your account history so you can see what your agents did. You can revoke our access at any time in the client portal or from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). To have all Google user data we hold deleted, email support@joinsmblab.com; we'll delete it within 30 days.

Limited Use. SMB Lab's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Microsoft (Outlook) data

If you connect a Microsoft account, we request permission to send email from your Outlook account and, if you turn on scheduling features, to read and update your calendar. We use and protect this data the same way we describe for Google user data in Section 5: only to provide the features you turned on, never for advertising or AI model training, and never sold. You can revoke access at any time in the portal or in your Microsoft account settings.

7. Mobile numbers and text messaging

SMB Lab's own texts. When you give us your mobile number through our website, the portal, or a conversation with us and agree to receive texts, we store the number along with a record of your consent, when it was given and how. We use it only to send the messages you agreed to, such as replies about your inquiry, demo reminders, account and service updates, assistant alerts and approvals, and information about SMB Lab.

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as our texting provider) that deliver messages for us.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text STOP to opt out (we also honor END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE and QUIT). You'll get one confirmation and no further texts. Opt-outs are honored within one business day, and we keep opt-out records for at least five years.
  • Text HELP for help, or email support@joinsmblab.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Texts sent for our Customers. When an agent texts you on behalf of a business that uses SMB Lab, that business is responsible for your number and consent record and for complying with texting laws, and we process that information for them. To stop those texts, reply STOP to that business's message or contact the business.

8. How we share information

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us run SMB Lab, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing services to us. These currently include:
    ProviderWhat they do for us
    RenderHosting for the client portal, agents and database
    NetlifyHosting for our website
    StripePayments and billing
    AnthropicAI models that run the agents
    OpenAICall and meeting transcription and AI models
    SlackCustomer workspace channels and agent messages
    We may add or change providers as the Services grow, and we'll update this list when we do.
  • With the services a Customer connects, when the Customer tells the agents to act in them (for example, writing a note to its CRM or sending a text through its phone system).
  • Within a Customer's team, with the people the Customer invites to its portal or Slack channel.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of SMB Lab, our Customers or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

9. How long we keep information

  • Customer Data is kept while the Customer's subscription is active. After a subscription ends, we delete Customer Data from our active systems within 30 days, and it ages out of backups within 90 days. Customers should export anything they want to keep before their last paid day.
  • Connections (API keys and access tokens) are deleted when the account is disconnected or the subscription ends.
  • Account and billing records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes.
  • Text opt-out records are kept for at least five years.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit, encrypted storage of API keys and access tokens, access limited to people who need it, and separation of each Customer's data and credentials. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll notify you as required by law.

11. Your choices and rights

You can update your account information in the portal, disconnect connected accounts, unsubscribe from marketing emails, and opt out of texts at any time. Depending on where you live (including under Colorado, California and other state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising or profiling. We don't sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email support@joinsmblab.com. We'll verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. You can use an authorized agent, and we won't discriminate against you for using your rights. If your information is Customer Data, we'll direct your request to the business that controls it, or handle it under their instructions. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

12. Children

SMB Lab is a business service and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Where data is processed

SMB Lab is based in the United States, and we and our providers process data in the United States.

14. Changes to this Policy

We'll post updates here and change the "Last updated" date. If we make material changes, including any change to how we use Google user data, we'll notify account owners by email or in the portal before the change takes effect.
